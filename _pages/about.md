I am an artist and technologist exploring the intersections of code, ecology, and digital culture. With a background in frontend engineering and currently studying Bsc. Image & Media Technology in Utrecht, my work bridges the physical and digital worlds through sound, image, and interactivity.

I wouldn't describe myself as a creative coder, but a coder that's creative. From practical applications to interactive installations, I build experiences that invite curiosity and connection.

Ecology is an essential part of my practice. I incorporate nature sounds, biomaterials, and sustainable computing to explore new ways of engaging with technology while being mindful of our planet. Sometimes this means rethinking how we use digital tools; other times, it means getting my hands dirty.

I am concerned about the future of the internet. I advocate for a decentralized, user-first web—one that resists exploitation by big tech. Through my work, I strive to raise awareness and explore alternatives that empower individuals.

These threads—technology, ecology, and digital freedom—are inseparable from who I am. My practice is collaborative and open, always inviting others to question, create, and explore with me.
