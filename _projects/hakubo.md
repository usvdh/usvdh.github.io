In collaboration with Allard Hebbink.

Hakubo is the Japanese word for twilight, the fleeting beauty of evening light. This generative algorithmic audio-visual work is a collection of highly personal relics from our lives, from Allard's sailing childhood to Sen's time living on a sub-tropical island in Japan; Ishigaki. It is a work embracing transitions, nostalgia, and the passage of time.

Part of the <a href="https://error030records.bandcamp.com/">Error 030 records</a> clubnight at <a href="https://www.kabulagogo.nl/events/error030-0201">KABUL à GoGo</a>.
